Birria Ramen

Description

Level up your ramen with this flavorful and yummy birria ramen recipe. This is a perfect meal for when you want to indulge in something different and can be customized with your favorite toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch of beef birria
  • 4-6 cups of beef stock
  • 4 packages ramen noodles 3 oz. each discard seasoning packets
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 cup diced onions
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. In a large pot, bring the birria broth to a boil.
  2. Add the ramen noodles and cook according to package instructions.
  3. Stir in the shredded beef, cheese, onions, and tomatoes. Heat gently until the cheese is melted and the soup is heated through.
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Serve hot, topped with additional shredded beef and cheese if desired.
