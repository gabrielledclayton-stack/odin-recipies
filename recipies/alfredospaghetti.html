Million Dollar Spaghetti Alfredo

Description

This is a delicious switch up to your usual spaghetti recipie. It is yummy, cheesy, and fulfilling for the whole family.

Ingredients

Alfredo Sauce

  • 1 box spaghetti
  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Spaghetti Meat

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 cup diced onions
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Onion powder
  • Italian seasoning
  • Red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Oregano
  • Cumin

Steps

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté until fragrant.
  3. Stir in the heavy cream and bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder to taste.
  5. Add the grated Parmesan cheese and stir until the sauce is smooth and creamy.
  6. Toss the cooked spaghetti with the Alfredo sauce, ensuring it is evenly coated.
  7. Cook ground beef in a separate skillet with diced onions, minced garlic, and the seasonings listed above until fully cooked.
  8. In a pan, combine the cooked ground beef mixture with the Alfredo spaghetti, gently tossing to ensure even distribution. Then, top with parmesan cheese and fresh parsley.
  9. Bake the combined spaghetti and meat mixture in the oven at 350°F (175°C) for 15-20 minutes.
  10. Serve hot, topped with additional Parmesan cheese if desired.
