Loaded Baked Potato Soup

Description

This is a delicious and hearty loaded baked potato soup recipe. It is perfect for a cold day and can be customized with your favorite toppings.

Ingredients

  • 4 large potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup cooked bacon bits
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. In a large pot, combine the diced potatoes and chicken broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until the potatoes are tender, about 15-20 minutes.
  2. Using an immersion blender or a regular blender, puree the soup until smooth.
  3. Stir in the milk, sour cream, and shredded cheddar cheese. Heat gently until the cheese is melted and the soup is heated through.
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Serve hot, topped with cooked bacon bits and sliced green onions.
